## Candidate comparison and selection

| Candidate | Why it can change observation | Evidence / verifiability | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Attention competition and occlusion | Ask what competes with the intended subject and which boundaries make it legible, rather than starting with a grid | Original practitioner composition cases, scoped perception studies, an unrelated licensed night scene; attention predictions remain hypotheses | Select; active comparison and intentional trade-offs |
| Position, distance and camera orientation | Separate correlated shooting variables, distinguish observed projection from guessed camera settings | PPA methods, Banks et al. projection/perception research, Ed Gregory's actual same-person grid (confounds explicit), licensed cosplay transfer | Select; causal prediction and design of the next comparison |
| Face-relative light and ambient/flash contributions | Distinguish where shadows fall from how bright they are, then choose a lighting intervention | Jane Allan head-turn case, David Hobby ambient/flash sequence, licensed window portrait; final images do not reveal exact ratios | Select; predict changes and turn the diagnosis into a shooting decision |
| Pose, support and weight transfer | Connect body orientation, support choices and sustainable pose variants | Existing practitioner methods are useful, but “centre of gravity” biomechanics and controlled comparison need additional evidence | Defer this round; retain all current skills, no automatic extra course |

Selection is based on source quality, visible cases, complementary reasoning tasks and bounded implementation. It is not a claim these three are proven photographic threshold concepts or the owner's three weakest abilities.

## Teaching design and evidence limits

The conceptual-gateway label is an organisational hypothesis informed by threshold-concept literature, not a certification of an irreversible breakthrough. The pedagogy research memo preserves exact reading scopes and bibliographic corrections. Contrasting-cases research motivates observation before explanation; it does not remove the need for clear explanations. The expert/novice photography study is small and task-specific, not proof of universal gaze paths. Transfer research motivates a genuinely different case and recording reasons, not automatic pass/fail.

Reusable structure: a gateway links multiple existing skills, has ordered navigable chapters, source locators, rights-aware cases, optional observation/contrast/transfer answers, intentionally delayed analyses and independent explicit cognitive self-assessment. Each gateway's mechanism, counterexample, visual interpretation and next-shot task must be authored and reviewed individually. The three samples use different reasoning operations; a shared reader is not a shared lesson recipe.

The reader contains original Chinese teaching, not unlicensed full-text translations. Copyrighted professional photos are linked at their original cases. Licensed remote photos retain creator, source, licence and modification status. No private reference photos, database, visual decisions or existing truth markers are migrated. Reading, revealing analysis and saving an answer never auto-promote ability.

## State and interaction contract

Gateway records are optional under `gateways` in the existing private learning JSON; old `skills` records and six statuses remain unchanged. They share revision, lock and atomic writes. Strict known IDs, bounded answers and 409 conflicts apply. No background write is inferred from reading.

The dialog owns its opening focus/route/scroll and per-gateway draft. Closing or switching retains drafts in this tab; explicit save persists to private storage. Cognitive self-assessment has unassessed plus the six requested stages. Unknown/unreadable server state is not mastery. The wide desktop reader and fullscreen mobile reader keep source access, chapter navigation and return continuity.

Every editable skill form also retains the field values it actually loaded. Refreshing global state after a gateway conflict cannot silently authorise that stale form to overwrite another window's edits. A changed field requires explicit server-version loading; both skill and gateway drafts remain intact until that choice. This is the same ownership rule across teaching and old records, not a special case for a particular error message.

Only `/learning` permits images from the fixed Commons thumbnail directory in CSP. Script, connection and reference-app policies remain local. The content validator enforces the matching image directory and intrinsic dimensions; browser tests exercise the real policy with synthetic pixels, while actual external-photo QA is reported separately. Remote cold-load latency and original-site access remain external dependencies; loading/failure states are visible and source links remain available.

## Expansion after these three

Do not bulk-generate 247 lessons. Next select a bottleneck from actual owner use; gather a licensed new case and a professional contrast; author its distinctive reasoning activity; review photography, teaching and interaction separately; trial with the owner using an unseen case; add only after the evidence and experience justify it. A later user study would need baseline and delayed observations, multiple cases, recorded prompts and scoring agreement before any effectiveness claim. No agent answer substitutes for a learner.
