- `light-037` — SUPPORTED: Aldred’s authored text explicitly distinguishes short-duration high-speed flash from HSS and warns ambient contribution can leave a sharp flash image plus blur; Nikon Kubota describes HSS as rapid pulses during focal-plane shutter travel and power loss; Carpenter explains flash duration freezing when ambient is controlled. Written sources support the exact distinction. Their associated videos were not used. High is defensible for mechanism distinction and choosing by ambient/sync conditions.
- `post-composite-light-match` — SUPPORTED_WITH_SCOPE_LIMIT: Adobe directly states direction/shadow should match and light quality should be similar, including warm/cool and harsh/soft mismatch. That second source supports tonal diagnosis in the stated example, not a universal multi-light rule; current skill does not force that universal interpretation. Two distinct instructional lineages support the capability.
- `post-composite-perspective-scale` — SUPPORTED: Adobe states use the same point of view and similar scale; PHLEARN says to correct background perspective before other composite correction steps. - `mot-07` — SUPPORTED_WITH_EXERCISE_LIMIT: Nikon’s live article recommends deciding what moves, and gives a specific example: keep a flower still for the first 0.5 seconds during a 1-second exposure, then move it.
